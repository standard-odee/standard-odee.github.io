Score: 4/5

Tsumugi Akita Animation Lab's started out as a subcontracting studio, this is their second anime. However, it's apparent they have great talent working there. The animation is first grade, and not only in the sense of high frame rate and movement. The colors pallets change between cuts, the subjects deform, their expressions become exaggerated and flow, instead of static poses and subtle queues, we're treated to emotive animation. It makes the characters convincing, separating them from their archetypes. The plot and themes don't distinguish the anime by themselves, it's fairly run of the mill overpowered reborn protagonist makes easy work of threats to his way of life while collecting a circle of admirers. The villains though, are memorable, they are reminiscent of the demons from folklore, strange, unnatural, a disaster for humans, yet uninterested in their affairs, still they're not unsympathetic. The fight scenes (unsurprisingly) bring out the best animation, and cements the presence of the villains and the rest of the cast.

Now there is a caveat before you can enjoy this anime. The design of the main character fetishes little boys. The hips and thighs I conveniently hid on the cover, are hard to overlook. But if you can get past that, there's a really fun anime to enjoy. The fanservice gets toned down as the series progresses, and instead focuses on the girls.
