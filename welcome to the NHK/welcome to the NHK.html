Score: 4/5

Welcome to the N.H.K is one of a kind. It's a description of the NEET and Hikikimori lifestyle that many have lived through, or could see themselves live through. Rather than the chronicles of a courageous hero who overcomes the human condition it entertains the cowardly living of someone who just might not be strong enough to survive. This somehow precludes it from being a classic, it is not something you watch as a reminder of the noble human inside us. After the initial joy of finding a kindred soul wears off, it's a harsh reminder of a life lived in shame.

It's a comforting companion when you're facing the world alone; an anime rooted in a deep seated shame. It's an acknowledgment of failure, and to acknowledge it is to acknowledge my own failure. I can never bring myself to rewatch it, but I could not abandon such a good friend.

Published: November 12th 2023
