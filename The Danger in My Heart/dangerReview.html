Score: 4/5

It's just a really nice romantic comedy? Romance manga typically targets women. Though it isn't uncommon for shoujo and josei manga to have a large male following. Its usually a serious romance, with a superficial story of a perfect man with charming imperfections choosing the heroin. I don't find there is a tradition of male targeted romances; instead, love interests are perpetuated in every genre, as objects to be conquered. Applying these principles ends up with a self gratifying romance that can hardly be satisfactory. Harem manga are a quantifiable logical conclusion, increase the number of girls to increase the number of conquests.

If I had to pick one way, I'd say it is more targeted towards guys by virtue of, the nature of the fanservice, and the girl being almost perfect while our hero is weak, woefully unimpressive. Though it's these deficiencies that make him lacking as a self insert. In turn, his conquest is not shared with the viewer. The premise further rejects conquest as the intrinsic goal, there is no fantastical admiration for the most beautiful girl in school; instead the main character's twisted thoughts mull about murdering her, establishing a desire for conquest, but not a sexual one. The heroine also takes a different turn from the typical plot path, she's not waiting to be chosen, or proving herself worthy of it, or even coming to approve of the man who chose her. Really she has little regard for his choices, and asserts herself by choosing to talk to him. The story then starts from a blank slate, conquest is not predetermined, the heroin never asked to be chosen, but they chose each other. It's a balanced romantic comedy. Addendum May 31st 2025: There is a tradition of male targeted romances, they're called rom-coms. Recent experience has me think that rom-coms, including this one, aren't nearly as appealing to women as I thought.
