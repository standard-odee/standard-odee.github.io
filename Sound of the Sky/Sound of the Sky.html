Score: 4/5

In a world where war has torn apart human civilization to the point that technology has reverted to the early 20th century, a squad of girls hold down a fort at their country's border. The anime's location is based on a real life spanish town, which serves as a memorable backdrop. Its an excellent healing anime that doesn't neglect to include a plot. Rather than seeking to make you forget about the harsh hopeless world. It reminds you that "Even if the world ends someday... our future will go on right until that fateful moment."
